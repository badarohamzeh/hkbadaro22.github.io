Academic profile & research experience

Final-year Computer Science student at Jordan University of Science and Technology studying Artificial Intelligence and Computational Neuroscience, with research interests spanning neuro-adaptive systems, language-model interpretability, and human-centered AI. My work combines applied research in VR biofeedback, secure AI systems, and physiological signal analysis while also mentoring students and developing technical communication skills through teaching and research collaboration.

  • CL-PAVE (in progress): Closed-loop physiologically adaptive VR system for exposure therapy on Meta Quest 3S, using ECG and HRV biofeedback to adjust intensity in real time.
  • MRVision: VR anxiety-reduction environment for MRI settings, designed for real-world clinical and hospital deployment scenarios.
  • Cross-lingual emotion representation: Probing internal representations and behavioral signatures of emotion across languages in instruction-tuned LLMs.
